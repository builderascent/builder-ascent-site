FIELD INTEGRITY

Our Standard for Recommendations

Commercial jobsites are tough environments where flimsy tools and marketing gimmicks fail immediately. Every recommendation in our upcoming library must pass three filters:

1. Does it solve a real jobsite problem?

It must improve daily flow, reduce waiting, tighten documentation, eliminate rework, or protect the crew. We don't recommend novelty items.

2. Can it withstand commercial conditions?

Dust, mud, concrete, rain, and daily wear destroy sub-par tools. If it isn't durable enough for full-time commercial construction, it doesn't make the list.

3. Would we give it to our own crew?

Credibility matters more than inventory volume. If we would not personally purchase and issue it on our projects, it has no place on Builder Ascent.
