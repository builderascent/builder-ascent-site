ROTATING FIELD FOCUS

This Week on the Jobsite

Focus on one operational discipline. Improve it. Repeat.

Weekly Focus: Eliminate Waiting

Waiting kills production. When crews are standing around, a system broke down upstream:
  • Materials not staged or inspected ahead of time
  • No clear direction or ambiguous task boundaries
  • Poor trade sequencing and workspace interference
  • Lack of advance communication with trade partners
The Builder Ascent standard:
  • What should have been staged yesterday?
  • What constraint can I remove before tomorrow morning?
  • What is our immediate backup task if work stalls?
Download Daily Checklist (PDF)

Apply This Today

  • I planned work before starting
  • I confirmed materials were ready
  • I had a backup task ready
  • I communicated early with trades
  • I kept work moving safely
