Your data and signatures

  • Everything you enter is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to Builder Ascent, and there is no account.
  • The device copy is not a backup. Download or share the PDF to keep a record.
  • Each person types their own name and confirms an acknowledgement. Drawn signatures are captured on the device and are not identity-verified and are not a certified electronic signature. Whether they meet your company’s or client’s requirements is for them to decide.
  • Records show only the information that was selected or entered. Hazard and control lists are starting points, not complete lists — follow your site safety plan and project requirements.
