INTERACTIVE FIELD FORMS

Interactive field records you fill in on your phone or tablet, review with the crew, sign on the device and save as a clean PDF. Free, no login, and your data stays on your device.

Pick what you need to fill out. Everything works on a phone, saves as you go, and turns into a clean PDF you can send.

Safety Planning

Plan the work and the hazards before the crew starts, then have the crew sign on right on the device.

  • Daily Pre-Task Plan — today’s work, hazards and controls, PPE, permits and crew sign-on.
  • JHA / JSA — task steps with hazards, controls and who’s responsible.
  • Toolbox Talk — the safety meeting, with every attendee signing in on the device.
Open Safety Planning

Inspections

Check equipment and PPE point by point, record what needs fixing, and sign it on the device.

  • Equipment Inspection — pre-use check with a clear result: safe to use, needs repair, or do not use.
  • PPE Inspection — hard hats, eye and hearing protection, gloves, high-vis, respirators, boots and fall protection.
Open Inspections

Daily Report

The end-of-day foreman log, done on your phone in a few minutes and signed on the device.

  • Work, crew and equipment — what got done, what’s next, who and what was on site, with man-hours added up.
  • Weather, deliveries, delays, safety and quality — the record you need when questions come up later.
Open Daily Report

Daily Builder Checklist

Your own daily routine from the Daily Builder System. About 12 minutes a day, no signatures.

  • Morning Plan, Midday Check, End of Day Review — four quick questions each. Tick them off, add a note if it helps.
  • Track Your Growth — six honest checks and one thing to do better tomorrow.
Open Daily Builder Checklist

Incidents & Near Misses

Report it while it’s fresh. Facts only — no medical details are stored on the phone.

  • Incident Report — what happened, people involved, level of care, causes, immediate and preventive actions.
  • Near Miss Report — the close call, what could have happened, and the fix. The reporter’s name is optional.
Open Incident Reports

Project Logs

Living logs that stay open while the work moves. Add entries, close them out, and send a PDF or spreadsheet any time.

  • Punch List — items by location and trade, with open and overdue at a glance.
  • RFI Log — questions, who they went to, when the answer is needed, and the response.
  • Change Order Log — changes with cost and time impact and approval status.
  • Materials Log — deliveries, quantities, damage and where it’s stored.
Open Project Logs

Your data and signatures

  • Everything you enter is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to Builder Ascent, and there is no account.
  • The device copy is not a backup. Download or share the PDF to keep a record.
  • Each person types their own name and confirms an acknowledgement. Drawn signatures are captured on the device and are not identity-verified and are not a certified electronic signature. Whether they meet your company’s or client’s requirements is for them to decide.
  • Records show only the information that was selected or entered. Hazard and control lists are starting points, not complete lists — follow your site safety plan and project requirements.
